Why this exists

Researchers still lack a strong predictor of who moves from suicidal thoughts to suicidal behavior, and few studies follow cognition, sleep, and life events day to day in veterans. This prototype explores one thin slice of that problem: a brief cognitive check-in, paired with a short log of events and symptoms, each compared with a person's own baseline.

What it does not do

It does not assess suicide risk, make predictions, or replace clinical judgment. Its purpose is to make change over time visible so that researchers and clinicians can ask better questions.

Next steps

Ethics approval and a clinical safety plan; testing with clinicians to see whether they can tell what changed, understand why it was flagged, and know what to ask next; then a pilot with consenting participants.
